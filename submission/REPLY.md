Screenshots, from the public demo. You can open the same views with `?sample=six&job=1`, `?sample=six&job=2`, `?sample=four`, and `?sample=limits`.

1. Six-colour tabletop set, Job 1 of 2. The board plus three pawns load four toolheads and fit the official 270 mm bed. (fourload-six-colour.png, also the cover)
2. The same plate, Job 2. White and black tokens are held back so the first job stays within four colours. (fourload-six-colour-job2.png)
3. Four-colour badge plate, one job, all four slots filled. (fourload-four-colour.png)
4. Limits sample: a 300 mm panel and a five-colour part are blocked, ABS and a PA-CF style name are flagged against official lists, and typed settings above official maxima are called out. (fourload-limits.png)

How to try it in a minute:

- Open the demo URL.
- Use "Load a sample" if you want a prepared plate, or drop your own 3MF.
- Switch jobs with the tabs above the bed.
- Export JSON or a markdown loadout card if you want a copy you can take to the printer.

I will keep this reply updated if the demo URL changes, or if a U1 owner reports a file that the planner gets wrong.
